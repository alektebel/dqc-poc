# Plan

## Overview

Recommend a Spain-only consultancy white-label accelerator at **€30,000/year plus €12,000 per scoped SI engagement**, with a secondary **€60,000/year direct-SI license** and a **€15,000 paid pilot**. These are proposed commercial terms, justified initially by measurable reductions in regulatory interpretation, control authoring and evidence preparation—not by replacing an entire governance platform.

## Scope

- In: Approximately 10–15 Spanish SI purchasing accounts, approximately 73 LSIs and 3–5 prospective consultancy partners, treated as provisional planning inputs.
- In: Complete competitor mapping, packaging, segment economics, differentiation, ROI, channel strategy and pricing validation.
- In: GL/2017/16 launch scope; RDARR/BCBS 239 positioning; conditional Anejo IX/Circular 4/2017 expansion.
- Out: Global market sizing, implementation refactoring, guaranteed supervisory acceptance and revenue attributed to unavailable modules.
- Out: Claims of a shipped scheduler, incident-triage agent, production SAS compiler or automated amendment certification.

## Testing Strategy

- Obtain comparable Spanish quotes or anonymized invoices for Collibra, Soda Enterprise, Ataccama and incumbent consultancy work; record commercial scope.
- Require approved applicability, zero partial/TODO cells in the accepted scope, recall = 1.0 on applicable tests, clean-data checks and resolution of overbroad/decoy findings.
- Test actual generated SQL, citations and reviewed ES/EN equivalence. Harness self-tests alone do not establish generator quality.
- Include reviewer-authored holdout defects and validate a second bank/schema before claiming repeatability.
- Verify local operation with external networking blocked; a stub demonstration does not validate local-model generation quality.
- Measure total authoring, adaptation, review and evidence effort. Proposed commercial pass: at least 30% reduction **and** positive buyer-specific ROI.
- Validate willingness to pay through paid acceptance and conversion; measure vendor contribution margin before scaling.
- Current review verified matrix counts and repository boundaries; it did not establish passing generated-check recall or live-pilot economics.

## Risks

- **Self-critique: proposed prices lack Spanish purchase evidence.** Require paid conversion before treating licenses as forecast revenue.
- **Self-critique: savings assume repeat engagements and substantial avoided labour.** At low volume or high adaptation effort, partner economics can fail; measure both.
- **Self-critique: the strongest proposed moat is incomplete in this checkout.** Restrict launch claims and exclude unavailable SAS/amendment capabilities.
- Consultancies may protect billable work or build internally. Target fixed-fee pressure and prove reusable delivery value; contractual restrictions alone are not a moat.
- A small Spanish buyer pool creates concentration risk. Deduplicate accounts and align fixed costs with signed commitments.
- Synthetic evidence does not establish regulatory completeness or supervisory acceptance. Maintain scope exclusions and accountable expert review.
- Annual corpus maintenance may exceed fees, while amendment demand may be irregular. Price maintenance costs explicitly and test recurring schema/revalidation demand.
- Competitors may provide equivalent evidence through custom implementations. Validate differentiation in buyer demonstrations rather than claiming universal absence.

## Rollback Plan

- After two qualified annual-license rejections following successful pilots, test **€15K–€20K standalone packs** with bounded reruns.
- Use the **€24K base/€10K engagement** partner floor only with reduced support or prepaid volume.
- If partners fail to convert within 90 days of acceptance, prioritize direct SI at €60K; test a **€40K narrow tier** with one assisted evidence release.
- If OSS wins the execution decision, sell applicability, mutation-library maintenance and evidence preparation around that stack.
- If technical acceptance fails, withdraw the failed pack and retain the previously approved controls; remove certification claims until demonstrated.
- Reprice renewals or new scope only; preserve promised pilot credits and delivered-artifact rights.

## Edge Cases

- **LSIs:** Confirm corpus applicability before offering a pack; standardised lenders may need the unavailable Anejo IX module.
- **Hard no-egress banks:** Require verified local operation; cloud fallback is not an acceptable substitute without changed bank requirements.
- **Existing Collibra/Informatica estates:** Sell complementary evidence and SQL; ready-made connectors are not assumed.
- **Tourism exposure:** Treat as portfolio-specific assessment, not a distinct institutional segment.
- **Savings/rural cooperatives:** Price the actual purchasing/shared-service group and avoid duplicate member-bank licenses.
- **Cross-border SI groups:** Limit entitlements to contracted Spanish scope.
- **Multiple consultancy partners:** Register scope and define reviewer accountability, artifact ownership and fee attribution.
- **SAS-heavy production:** Require native bank-side execution validation; SQLite evaluation does not prove SAS equivalence.
- **No material regulatory amendment:** Renewal must still earn its price through agreed maintenance, schema changes and repeated validation.
